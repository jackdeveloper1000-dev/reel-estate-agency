10 Instagram Reels ideas for real-estate agents

Updated 23 November 2026 · 3 min read

Reels are one of the easiest ways for an agent to be seen by people who don’t follow you yet. The hard part is knowing what to post every week. Here are ten ideas you can repeat with every new listing, plus a few habits that make any Reel more effective.

1. The 30-second walkthrough

Take the listing photos, put them in the order of a visit and turn them into a vertical film with slow camera moves. Put the locality and configuration on screen in the first second. This is your bread-and-butter Reel – post one for every listing.

2. Guess the price

Show the highlights of a home and ask viewers to guess the price in the comments, then reveal it in the last seconds or in a reply. Questions invite comments, and comments can help a Reel reach more people.

3. Three things I love about this home

Pick three specific features – the morning light in the living room, the separate utility area, the view from the balcony – and give each one its own shot with a short caption. Specific details feel more honest than generic words like “luxurious” or “spacious”.

4. Locality guide

Buyers buy a neighbourhood as much as a home. Film a short tour of what is nearby: the metro station, schools, hospitals, markets and parks, with the travel time to each on screen. These Reels stay useful long after a listing is sold.

5. What your budget buys

Compare what a fixed budget gets you in two or three localities – size, configuration and amenities. It answers a question every buyer has and positions you as someone who knows the market.

6. Site-visit checklist

Share practical tips: check water pressure and the direction of sunlight, look for damp patches, ask about maintenance charges and parking, and ask to see approvals and the RERA registration for new projects. Helpful tips get saved and shared, which keeps your name in front of buyers.

7. Just sold or just rented

Celebrate a closed deal with a short film of the property and a thank-you to the client – with their permission. It is social proof that you get results.

8. A day of site visits

Behind-the-scenes Reels show the person behind the listings: the early start, the drive, the keys, the walk-throughs. People prefer to call an agent they feel they already know.

9. Client stories

A short clip of a happy buyer or landlord talking about their experience is more convincing than anything you can say about yourself. Always ask for consent and never script their words.

10. Answer one question

Take a question you hear every week – “Is it better to buy ready-to-move or under-construction?”, “What documents should a tenant ask for?” – and answer it in under 45 seconds. Keep it general and suggest professional advice for legal or tax questions.

Habits that make any Reel work harder

  • Hook in the first two seconds. Lead with the best shot or the most surprising fact, not your logo.
  • Add on-screen text. Many people watch with the sound off.
  • Shoot and edit vertically. A 9:16 video fills the whole screen; see our video sizes guide.
  • End with one clear next step – “DM ‘VISIT’ to book a viewing” works better than a list of options.
  • Tag the location and use a few relevant hashtags for the locality and property type.
  • Reply quickly to comments and messages – a fast reply is often what turns a viewer into a lead.
  • Be consistent. Two or three Reels every week beats ten in one week and nothing for a month.
