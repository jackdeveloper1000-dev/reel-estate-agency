About us

Property films made from the photos you already have

ESTAMOTION turns the photos from a property listing into a short, cinematic Full-HD film that is ready to post on property portals, YouTube, Instagram and WhatsApp. It is built for real-estate agents, builders, landlords and owners who want their listing to stand out without hiring a videographer.

How it works

  • Get the images. Paste the link of a property listing and ESTAMOTION collects its photos. No link? Upload the photos from your phone or computer instead.
  • Choose and style. Pick the photos you want and put them in order, check the address, price and agent details, then choose the format, transitions, colour grade, camera motion and music.
  • Make the film. Each photo becomes a smooth camera shot, the shots are joined with transitions, and titles, a letterbox, music and your branded end card are added.
  • Download and share. You get an MP4 file you can download and post anywhere.

What goes into every film

  • Eased camera moves – push-ins, pans and tilts – so still photos feel like video
  • A warm golden colour grade and an optional cinema letterbox
  • A title with the property address and price
  • An end card with the agent’s name, phone number and agency
  • A built-in royalty-free music score, your own track, or no music
  • Formats for every platform: landscape 16:9, vertical 9:16 for Reels and Shorts, portrait 4:5, square 1:1 and 21:9 cinema

Only what is really there

Every film is made from the real listing photos. ESTAMOTION moves the camera, grades the colour and adds titles – it does not invent rooms, furniture or views that are not in the photos, so buyers and tenants see the property as it is.

Pricing

Collecting and arranging photos is free. You pay once per film when you press Make Film, and the price is shown before you pay. If a film fails to render you can retry it at no extra cost.

Your films stay private

Only you can see, play and download the films in your account. After a film is made, the listing photos and any music you uploaded are deleted from our servers; only the finished film is kept for you to download. Read our privacy policy for the details.
