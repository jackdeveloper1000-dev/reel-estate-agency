Privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

This policy explains what information ESTAMOTION (“we”, “us”) collects when you use our website and app, why we collect it, who we share it with and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Account details. Your name and mobile number. We use your number to send you a one-time code by SMS so you can log in – we do not use passwords.
  • What you give us to make a film. Listing links you paste, the photos we collect from those links or that you upload, property details such as address and price, the agent name, phone number and agency shown on the end card, and any music you upload.
  • Payments. Payments are processed by Razorpay. We keep a record of each payment (amount, status and Razorpay reference), but we never see or store your full card, UPI or bank details.
  • Technical information. Like most websites, our servers and hosting provider may record basic details such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you request, to keep the service secure and fix problems.

How we use your information

  • To log you in and keep your account secure
  • To collect your listing photos and make, store and deliver your films
  • To take payments and keep payment records
  • To send you service messages by SMS, such as your login code or a note that your film is ready
  • To prevent abuse and keep the service running
  • To show advertising on our website and app (see “Advertising” below)

Cookies and similar technologies

  • Login cookie. When you log in we set a cookie that keeps you signed in for up to 30 days. Without it, logging in would not work.
  • Session storage. If you paste a link before logging in, your browser remembers it for that visit so you don’t have to paste it again.
  • Advertising cookies and IDs. Our website shows ads from Google AdSense, which uses cookies, and our app shows ads from Google AdMob, which uses your device’s advertising ID – see below.

Advertising

We use Google AdSense to show ads on parts of our website. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this website and other websites. Google’s use of advertising cookies lets Google and its partners show you ads based on your visits to our site and/or other sites on the internet.

  • You can turn off personalised advertising from Google in Google Ads Settings.
  • You can opt out of personalised ads from other participating third-party vendors at www.aboutads.info.
  • To learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Our mobile app shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may use your device’s advertising ID and similar information to show and measure ads. You can reset your advertising ID or turn off personalised ads in your phone’s privacy or ads settings.

We do not show ads on the login, film-editing or payment screens.

Who we share information with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with the service providers that help us run ESTAMOTION:

  • our hosting provider, which stores the website, database and your films
  • our SMS provider, which delivers login codes and notifications to your phone
  • Razorpay, which processes payments
  • Google, which serves the ads on our website and app

We may also share information if the law requires it or to protect our users and our service.

How long we keep it

  • After a film is made successfully, its listing photos and any uploaded music are kept for up to 7 days so you can export additional aspect-ratio cuts (such as vertical Reels), and are then automatically deleted. You can also remove source photos immediately after rendering from the film page. The finished film (MP4) and its preview image stay in your account until you delete the film.
  • Your account details are kept while your account is active.
  • Payment records are kept for as long as accounting and tax laws require.

Keeping it safe

Each film can only be seen, played and downloaded by the account that made it. Logins use one-time SMS codes and a login cookie that scripts on the page cannot read. No method of storing or sending data over the internet is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

Your choices

  • Delete any film at any time from My Films.
  • Ask us for a copy of your information, to correct it, or to delete your account by contacting us.
  • Control advertising cookies with the links in “Advertising” above, or block cookies in your browser settings.

Children

ESTAMOTION is meant for adults (18+) and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy
